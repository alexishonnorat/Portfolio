Concept

Two tracks. One dataset problem.

Legal and content intelligence, assembled from sources that were never meant to be queried together.

The problem

Legal and HR data is public but scattered — thousands of articles across Légifrance, URSSAF rules with no standard format, and court decisions locked behind undocumented APIs. Meanwhile, PayFit's content strategy has no benchmark: no dataset maps keyword coverage against Factorial, Lucca, Cegid or Sage.

The solution

A Python pipeline in two complementary tracks: a legal data ingestion layer connecting to Légifrance, URSSAF and Judilibre via PISTE APIs, and a content intelligence layer crawling PayFit and four competitors to extract themes, keyword density and editorial positioning.

Overview

From public sources to exploitable datasets.

The project is split into two complementary tracks: official legal data recovery and SEO-oriented content intelligence.

01

Collect

PISTE-authenticated API clients hit Légifrance (~21 000 articles), URSSAF (1 334 rules, no auth) and Judilibre (court decisions by theme). Crawl4AI and Playwright scrape PayFit articles by category and sitemap, plus four competitors.

02

Normalize

Heterogeneous API responses and scraped HTML are flattened into structured Pandas DataFrames. Resume flags handle long-running extractions; JSON-to-CSV converters produce downstream-ready files.

03

Analyze

Keywords ranked by theme, competitor coverage mapped across Factorial, Lucca, Cegid and Sage. Article metadata (author, reading time, structure) feeds into editorial positioning reports.

04

Activate

Outputs land in output/ as JSON, CSV and Markdown reports. Legal datasets feed research workflows; keyword reports identify SEO/GEO gaps directly actionable for content strategy.

SEO / GEO intelligence

Crawl PayFit and benchmark competitors.

A dedicated scraping layer explores PayFit practical sheets and competitor blogs to identify themes, coverage and keyword opportunities.

PayFit content crawl

Category-based and sitemap-based crawling of PayFit practical articles with Crawl4AI and Playwright. Extracts title, metadata, reading time, author and article structure. Feeds into keyword density and topical coverage analysis.

Competitor benchmark

Parallel extraction workflows for Factorial, Lucca, Cegid and Sage. Each competitor's blog and help content is scraped for topical coverage, editorial frequency and keyword overlap with PayFit — surfacing gaps and over-indexed themes.

Keyword reports

Keywords ranked by theme and volume of coverage. Outputs reusable Markdown and JSON reports ready to plug into a content strategy document or GEO brief. Identifies which topics have no coverage and which are saturated across all players.

Legal data

Build a reusable legal knowledge base.

The legal pipeline gathers official references from French public APIs and exports them into local structured files.

Légifrance

Full or limited export of Code du travail articles (~21 000) via PISTE OAuth. Supports --limit for quick validation, --resume to restart interrupted runs, configurable --delay and custom output paths. Exports to JSON and CSV.

URSSAF

Collection of 1 334 mon-entreprise rules without authentication — the simplest pipeline in the project. Exported to JSON and CSV for direct downstream use. No rate-limiting issues; ideal as a stable reference dataset.

Judilibre

Court decisions retrieved by employment-law theme (dismissal, paid leave…) via PISTE OAuth or a dedicated API key. 5 decisions per theme by default, extensible. Output: articles_judilibre.json.

Architecture

Focused Python tooling.

Public APIs, authenticated scraping and Pandas pipelines — minimal dependencies, maximum data yield.
