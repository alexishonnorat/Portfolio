Concept

One flow for every outing.

Together replaces ten message threads with a single, coherent decision flow.

The problem

Organizing an outing means scattered threads across three apps, half-committed answers and no single source of truth for who is actually coming. Decisions dissolve in the noise before anyone commits.

The solution

One place for everything: propose, vote, confirm, RSVP and remember. Designed mobile-first with a native app feel — tab navigation, stories, social feed and maps. Close to an iOS/Android experience in a web app.

User workflow

From idea to shared moment.

Together structures the messy social process of planning an outing into a clear sequence.

01

Discuss

Groups are the social unit. Each group has a dedicated chat where members attach proposals, vote on outings, and coordinate without leaving the thread. Realtime Supabase subscriptions keep every participant in sync as decisions evolve.

02

Vote

When someone proposes an outing, the group votes before it becomes confirmed. Members compare options, approve or decline, and the system surfaces the winning proposal. The vote-to-confirmation flow prevents half-committed plans.

03

RSVP

Confirmed outings collect individual attendance. Members mark themselves going, maybe or out — the organizer sees a live headcount. The group stays aligned without spawning a separate message thread for logistics.

04

Remember

After the outing, Moments, a social feed and Stories transform shared experiences into persistent group memory. Photos and reactions create continuity between outings and give the app a reason to exist beyond logistics.

Features

Everything in one place.

Six product surfaces, each solving a specific friction point in group planning.

Groups & Chat

Realtime messages via Supabase Realtime, history persisted in PostgreSQL. Proposals and activities are attached to conversation context so plans stay connected to the discussion that produced them.

Outing workflow

Proposal → vote → confirmation → RSVP → moment sharing. The vote-to-confirmation step prevents half-committed plans. Everyone knows the status without asking.

Feed & Stories

Photo posts, likes, comments and sharing. Stories transform shared experiences into persistent group memory and give the app a reason to exist between outings.

Map & Places

Leaflet maps with restaurant, bar and activity datasets for Paris. "Open only" filter, price/person info. Pin a spot and share it directly into a group proposal without leaving the app.

Social profiles

Public/private accounts, follow graph, outing history. Light social discovery — the feed surfaces what friends are planning, not just groups you're already in.

Recommendations

AI-powered place suggestions via OpenRouter/OpenAI based on group preferences, budget filter and location. Multi-option vote to let the group choose between AI proposals.
